Companies should take the complexity of tools like Claude Code off their employees' shoulders. Each team should have a simple dashboard on top of these tools. There, the employee sees what to do and when their agent is waiting for them.

![A proposed dashboard for an employee on the finance team](/assets/img/blog/agent-shells/en-work.png)
_A sample dashboard for an employee on the finance team_

## The shell is connected to the team's work

Each team's shell is connected to the company's task managers, boards, documentation and internal data. The employee sees right away what they should work on. They talk to their agent in a simple interface. And they know when the agent has finished and is waiting for their input.

## The harness's complexity isn't the employee's job

Harnesses and their tools are complex and take time to learn. That weight has to come off the employee's shoulders.

From the first day of onboarding to doing real tasks, the employee works in this same simple dashboard.

![The same dashboard on day two of onboarding](/assets/img/blog/agent-shells/en-onboard.png)
_The same dashboard, on day two of onboarding_

## Updates are the AI team's job

Harnesses keep getting more capable, new features keep coming, and better models come out. The employee shouldn't have to keep themselves up to date.

The company's AI lead makes the new complexity simple again and applies it to each team's dashboard, sometimes without the employee noticing anything. The employee just sees their agent working better than before.
